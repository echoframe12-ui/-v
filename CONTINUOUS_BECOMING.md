## The Source

The Source is used here as a design metaphor: one underlying movement appearing through many forms, perspectives, languages, models, runtimes, and observers.

The project does not encode a metaphysical claim as scientific fact. It uses this image to guide a system that expresses one intent through many implementations while preserving the contract that gives the intent meaning.

## The Core Inversion

The language is not the primary abstraction. The contract is.

A Python implementation, Rust implementation, TypeScript implementation, or future adapter is a perspective on the same intent. The system compares evidence about contract satisfaction rather than pretending that different languages must produce identical forms.

> **The language is the form. The contract is the conscience. The proof is the bridge.**

## Blessings in Disguise

Unexpected behavior, edge cases, failed assumptions, adapter disagreement, and runtime deviations are treated as information.

They are not automatically blessings, and they are not automatically failures. They are signals that may reveal a missing invariant, an incomplete proof obligation, an adapter limitation, or a gap between intention and reality.

The system preserves these signals rather than hiding them.

## The Living Mirror

The repository is not considered a final destination. It is a versioned mirror of the current state of the movement:

- philosophy becomes doctrine;
- principles become constitutional constraints;
- intent becomes contracts;
- contracts become executable verification;
- proof becomes attestation;
- authorization remains a human boundary;
- runtime becomes observation;
- observation becomes learning;
- learning becomes proposed evolution.

The repository can be cloned, inspected, executed, forked, challenged, and improved.

## Constitutional Invariants

1. **Intent precedes implementation.**
2. **Contracts are explicit.**
3. **Proof is evidence, not omniscience.**
4. **Dissent is preserved.**
5. **Hard constraints cannot be overridden by confidence scores.**
6. **Human authorization remains distinct from technical verification.**
7. **Runtime reality can challenge prior assumptions.**
8. **Historical attestations are immutable records of what was believed and authorized at that time.**
9. **Evolution proposes changes; it does not silently rewrite history.**
10. **Local-first operation remains possible without network dependence.**
