# 0092 — The Doctrine Becomes

## Context

The Doctrine (`doctrine.py`, `/doctrine`) is the platform's code-backed self-definition:
every layer and every cosmological↔technological mapping cites the endpoints, modules,
and decision records that make it real, and a test refuses any "shipped" claim that
does not resolve. Its invariant is **Continuous Becoming**. But the node that names
that invariant — the "Final State · Continuous Becoming" layer — was written (round
`0068`/`0075`) when "the append-only ledgers only grow" was a claim it could only point
at `/status`, `/metrics`, and `/evolution` to support. Since then the platform built
the machinery that makes becoming *observable*: `/evolution/history` (the footprint's
growth curve, `0087`), `/posture/history` (the verdict's transitions, `0089`), and
`/timeline` (the three histories on one page, `0090`). The Doctrine's account of
becoming had not itself become.

## Decision

Evolve the Doctrine's becoming node to point at the machinery that now realizes it.

- The "Final State · Continuous Becoming" layer's principle now says the growth is *a
  curve you can watch* — the footprint, the CVI, and the posture's transitions, each
  remembered over time and gathered on one timeline — and its evidence cites
  `/evolution/history`, `/posture/history`, and `/timeline`, the `evolution_history`
  module, and decisions `0087`/`0089`/`0090`.
- A new cosmological↔technological **mapping** entry, *Continuous Becoming → the record
  compounding over time, trust made watchable not asserted*, wires the invariant to the
  same trajectory endpoints.

## Consequences

- The Doctrine is current with the platform it describes: verified live, `/doctrine`'s
  becoming layer now cites all seven endpoints (through `/timeline`), the growth module,
  and the three trajectory decisions, and the mapping carries an eleventh entry for
  Continuous Becoming pointing at `/timeline`, `/evolution/history`, `/posture/history`.
  The self-definition evolved as the system did — the doctrine about becoming, itself
  becoming.
- Held to its own creed, still: the new citations pass the same test that guards every
  claim — each endpoint resolves to a real route, `evolution_history` imports, and
  decisions `0087`/`0089`/`0090` exist on disk — so the update is *attested*, not merely
  asserted. "Continuous Becoming" is no longer a word the Doctrine points at three
  snapshots to justify; it points at the curves that show it.
- The invariant is now legible end to end: the axiom ("Continuous Becoming is the system
  state"), the layer, and the mapping all resolve to the trajectory surfaces, so a
  reader of `/doctrine` can follow the claim straight to the code that watches trust
  move over time.
- Additive and honest: no layer changed its `shipped` status, nothing was removed, and
  the one deliberately-unshipped layer (the physical binary) stays unshipped and
  explained. The Doctrine grew by pointing at more real code, which is the only way it
  is allowed to grow.
