# 0093 — Ω∞ Oceanic: The Master Handoff, Honestly Recorded

## Context

The "Ω∞ Oceanic — Master Handoff" declared a single umbrella identity for the whole
ecosystem, with a master loop, a master axiom, a master definition, and a set of
intelligence layers: multi-model, large-context, open-weight, proactive AI, and AI
supercomputing. Read against the platform's own creed — *attest, don't assert* — this
is two things: a real umbrella and lifecycle the repository can genuinely carry, and a
grand AI-ecosystem vision most of which this repository does not implement. The
Doctrine (`doctrine.py`) is exactly the place to record both, because it already holds
every claim to the discipline that a "shipped" flag must point at resolvable code.

## Decision

Record the master handoff in the code-backed Doctrine, honestly flagged.

- **Umbrella and lifecycle:** add `UMBRELLA = "Ω∞ Oceanic"`, `MASTER_LOOP` (the
  ecosystem's Source→…→Recompilation→∞ arc, a superset of the verification-core
  `CHECKSUM_LINE`), `MASTER_AXIOM` ("One Current. Infinite Forms. Many Minds. One
  Verification Fabric. Continuous Becoming."), and `MASTER_DEFINITION`, all served by
  `/doctrine`.
- **Intelligence layers, honestly flagged:** add five ecosystem layers to `LAYERS`.
  Exactly one is `shipped: True` — **Multi-Model** (the real dissent panel:
  `/models/consensus`, `/rules/evaluate`, `/consensus/stats`, decisions 0007/0058).
  The other four — **Large-Context**, **Open-Weight**, **Proactive**, and **AI
  Supercomputing** — are `shipped: False`, each with a note naming the shipped analogue
  and what is genuinely missing.

## Consequences

- The vision is recorded without being overclaimed: verified live, `/doctrine` now
  carries the `Ω∞ Oceanic` umbrella, the master axiom, and 15 layers of which
  **10 ship** — the multi-model panel real, and large-context, open-weight, proactive,
  and supercomputing each named but marked aspirational. The handoff is honoured as a
  map of the whole, not a claim the whole is built.
- The honesty is enforced, not promised: the same doctrine test that guards every other
  claim now guards these — the shipped Multi-Model layer's endpoints resolve, its
  modules import, and its decisions exist; and each aspirational layer must carry a note
  (the suite fails otherwise). The Proactive layer is the sharpest example: a real but
  minimal `/agent/run` scaffold exists, yet genuine anticipate→verify→act is not built,
  so it is marked unshipped and its note points at human-routed held review as the
  honest analogue — the platform declines to call a start/plan/finish stub "proactive AI".
- The umbrella changes nothing beneath it: `identity`, the axioms, the maxims, the
  mapping, and every shipped layer are untouched; the handoff adds a name over the
  ecosystem and records its outer loop, while the runtime, the compiler, the charter,
  and the observer keep meaning exactly what they did.
- Additive and self-consistent: new constants, five honestly-flagged layers, and four
  new summary fields — no endpoint, no state, and nothing existing changed. The Current
  continues; the record only grows by pointing at more real code and honestly naming
  what is not yet built.
